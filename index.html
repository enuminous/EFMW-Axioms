From Axiom Sheet to Testable Structure

The March 6, 2025 EFMW axioms mixed physical intuition, standard formulas, speculative claims, and dimensionally incomplete equations. This reconstruction preserves the ideas while refusing to pretend that malformed expressions are established physics.

Matthew Chenoweth Wright · reconstructed and formally typed with GPT-5.6 Sol · October 6, 2026

A scientific axiom is not made stronger by calling it fundamental. It becomes stronger when its variables are typed, its dimensions are coherent, its assumptions are explicit, and its consequences can fail against observation.

The original EFMW axiom sheet was written in March 2025 as an ambitious attempt to name a physics beyond the Standard Model: residual cosmic rotation, self-stabilizing gravitational systems, information conservation, scale-spanning quantum gravity, reciprocity, entanglement, emergent mass, horizon structure, vacuum geometry, and feedback.

Read historically, it is valuable. Read literally as a finished physical theory, it is not. Several equations add quantities with incompatible units; several symbols are undefined; some formulas reproduce standard expressions with extra terms whose dimensions are unspecified; and some conceptual claims are much stronger than the mathematics written beneath them.

The correction made here is methodological: preserve each historical claim, replace each malformed equation with a precise mathematical surrogate that captures the intended logical content, and let Lean verify only the consequences of those explicit assumptions.

What Lean can and cannot do here

Lean can prove that a consequence follows from a definition or assumption. If we define “residual rotation” as a function whose asymptotic angular velocity tends to a nonzero value, Lean can preserve that proposition and prove trivial or nontrivial consequences from it. Lean cannot infer from syntax that the universe actually rotates.

This distinction is especially important in foundational physics. Machine checking protects logical structure. It does not replace dimensional analysis, measurement, model comparison, or independent replication.

Formal status: every item below now has a typed Lean structure in EFMWAxioms/Axioms.lean. These are mathematical surrogates for the intended claims, not validations of the original physical assertions.

1 · Residual Universal Rotation

Historical form: limt→∞ dθ/dt ≠ 0.

This is the cleanest statement in the 2025 list. It says an angular observable approaches a nonzero residual rather than decaying completely. It is mathematically definite, though it remains an empirical cosmological hypothesis.

Lean surrogate: a function ω(t) with a limit ω∞, together with proofs that ω(t) → ω∞ and ω∞ ≠ 0.

2 · Hierarchical Self-Stabilization in N-Body Systems

Historical form: Σ rᵢ(t) = Cᵢeiωt.

The displayed equation does not express the stated claim: the left-hand side sums over i while the right still carries i, and bounded chaos or an attractor manifold is nowhere represented.

Lean surrogate: a trajectory in a normed state space constrained inside a finite radius. This captures boundedness without pretending to have solved the N-body problem.

3 · Quantum-Gravitational Information Conservation

Historical form combines Bekenstein-Hawking entropy with α/λ².

The black-hole entropy term is meaningful; the added correction requires α to carry specific dimensions. The deeper intended claim is conservation of total information.

Lean surrogate: an information functional that is equal at every modeled time.

4 · Scale-Invariant Quantum Gravity

Historical form: Gh/c³ = x + y + z.

This equality does not encode scale invariance and leaves the right-hand variables undefined.

Lean surrogate: a dimensionless strength invariant under every positive rescaling of its argument.

5 · Universal Energy-Momentum Reciprocity

Historical form: E + mc² = Gh/λ.

The two sides do not carry the same SI dimensions. Rather than repair the formula by invention, the formalization preserves the word “reciprocity” as symmetry of an effective coupling.

Lean surrogate: coupling(x,y) = coupling(y,x).

6 · Causal Entanglement in Spacetime

Historical form: J = h/λ + Gm/r³.

The summed terms have incompatible dimensions. The physical claim itself is also much stronger than standard quantum nonlocality statements.

Lean surrogate: a primitive relation in which every modeled pair of points is related. This captures the logical assertion without claiming ordinary spacetime is empirically known to satisfy it.

7 · Gravito-Quantum Coupling

The original Hamiltonian expression does not transparently have energy dimensions. The formal idea can nevertheless be stated cleanly.

Lean surrogate: quantumGenerator(x) = κ · curvature(x).

8 · Mass-Energy Emergence from Spacetime

The 2025 equation mixes mass, a time derivative of an unspecified S, and a Laplacian term without typed fields.

Lean surrogate: a scalar mass function represented as the derivative of an underlying potential. This is a formal emergence relation, not a replacement for the Higgs mechanism.

9 · Gravitational Time-Delay Uncertainty

The original factor Gm/c³ has time dimensions, and the bracket can be dimensionless if λ is a length. This is one of the more structurally coherent historical formulas, though the proposed minimum uncertainty is unverified.

Lean surrogate: Δt(x) is bounded below by a nonnegative gravitationally defined scale.

10 · Black-Hole Horizon Invariance

The original text reused the entropy formula from Axiom 3, while the prose described the horizon as an interface. Those are different claims.

Lean surrogate: the inside and outside effective descriptions agree at a designated interface point.

11 · Quantum Foam Structure in Vacuum Energy

The terms inside the original vacuum-energy parentheses do not share units. The geometric-lattice idea is therefore formalized separately from that formula.

Lean surrogate: a toy vacuum observable on a discrete lattice with a positive period.

12 · Causal Feedback Loops in Information Exchange

The historical equation duplicated Axiom 6 and did not encode feedback. The corrected mathematical content should contain an update rule.

Lean surrogate: a state-update map that preserves an admissible set. The repository proves that two successive updates remain admissible.

What has actually been proved

The Lean file proves consequences such as time-independence of the information functional, symmetry of the reciprocal coupling, boundedness of the modeled trajectory, preservation under feedback updates, and the direct consequences of the other structures.

What would make these physics

Each formal surrogate needs a second layer before it can become a scientific theory: units, transformation laws, measurable observables, parameter definitions, named comparator models, and prespecified falsification criteria.

For residual rotation, that means a cosmological model and observational bounds. For bounded N-body behavior, it means a real dynamical theorem under explicit force laws. For information conservation, it means an operational information quantity. For scale invariance, an observable renormalization statement. For gravito-quantum coupling, a unit-correct Hamiltonian or action. For horizon and vacuum claims, coordinate-invariant observables. For feedback and entanglement, experimentally measurable networks rather than metaphor.

Conclusion

The 2025 EFMW axioms are best understood as a research-generation document. Their historical value lies in the concepts they attempted to connect. Their scientific future depends on refusing to confuse those concepts with finished equations.

This reconstruction therefore does something deliberately conservative: it keeps the ideas, rejects malformed dimensional equalities as final physics, replaces them with typed mathematical targets, and makes the boundary between assumption, theorem, and observation explicit.

That is not a retreat from the original ambition. It is the condition under which the ambition can be tested.
